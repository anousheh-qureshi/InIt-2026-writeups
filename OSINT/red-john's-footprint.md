# 🚩 Red John's Footprint

**Category:** OSINT  
**Points:** 100  
**Challenge Name:** Red John's Footprint

## 📝 Challenge Hint

The hint provided was:

> rEdJ0Hn203 – The Accounting

## 🔍 Solution / Approach

### 1. Finding the GitHub Account

I started by analysing the challenge hint and searched GitHub for the account:

**`rEdJ0Hn203`**

After finding the account, I noticed a pinned folder containing two files:

- `for_patrick`
- `rEdJ0Hn.md`

### 2. Investigating the Files

First, I opened `for_patrick` and examined its contents, but I couldn't find anything useful.

Next, I checked `rEdJ0Hn.md`, where I found a link titled **"Read My Story."**

I followed the link, which led me to a story titled:

**"The Man Who Drank Tea and Forgot to Be Angry."**

### 3. Analysing the Story

I carefully read through the story and searched for hidden clues.

There were multiple decoy flags throughout the content. I tried them, but none of them worked.

Instead of giving up, I continued examining the story and paid closer attention to the smaller details.

### 4. Discovering the Hidden Flag

While going through the instructions on **how to make a proper cup of tea**, I noticed something interesting in the third step.

That particular step contained the actual flag.

## 🏁 Flag

```text
INLT{th3_truth_w4s_1n_th3_w4t3r}
```

## 💡 Key Takeaway

This challenge showed the importance of patience and attention to detail in OSINT.

Not every flag-looking string is necessarily the correct answer. Sometimes, the real clue is hidden in ordinary-looking content, and carefully examining the entire source makes all the difference.

**The truth was in the water!** ☕🔎
